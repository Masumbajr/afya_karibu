Hospitali Online is a free Android app that helps you find health facilities in Tanzania. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Your location stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with information.

> **Not for emergencies.** Hospitali Online is a directory. It is not medical advice and it is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency services or go to the nearest health facility straight away. Do not wait for a reply from us or from the app.

## 1. What stays on your phone

- **The facility directory.** The list of facilities is built into the app, so searching and browsing work without an internet connection.
- **Your location.** If you allow the location permission, the app uses your phone's location to show facilities near you and your position on the map. This is worked out on your phone. We do not receive, store or send your location. You can refuse or withdraw the permission in your Android settings, and search will still work.
- **Your searches.** What you search for is handled on your phone and is not sent to us.

## 2. What you send us

You can suggest a correction or a new facility. If you do, the app sends what you type into the form (for example the facility's name, location and details, and any contact detail you choose to add) to our database hosted on Google Firebase. Google also receives technical information that comes with any internet request, such as your IP address.

We use submissions only to check them and, if they are verified, to update the directory. Our moderator at DayBridge Enterprises Ltd reviews every submission before anything is published. We never publish your contact details. Please do not put your own health information, or anyone else's, in a submission.

## 3. Other services the app uses

- **OpenStreetMap (maps).** The map pictures are loaded from OpenStreetMap's servers. Like any website or app that loads a picture, their servers can see your IP address and which part of the map you are viewing. See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.
- **Google Firebase (submissions).** If you send a submission, it is stored and processed by Google. See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with anyone other than the services above.

## 4. Where information is processed

Google's servers, and those of the map provider, may be outside Tanzania. If you send a submission, it is processed outside Tanzania.

## 5. How long we keep information

Submissions are kept until we have checked them. Once a submission has been added to the directory or rejected, we delete your contact detail, if you gave one, within 90 days. You can ask us to delete a submission earlier (see section 6). Everything the app stores on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

## 6. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 of Tanzania, you may ask us whether we hold personal information about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. To do any of these, contact us using the details above. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania.

## 7. Security

Submissions are sent to Firebase over an encrypted connection. We limit who can see them to our moderator and the people who maintain the app. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children

Hospitali Online is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Accuracy of the directory

The facility information comes from [DATA SOURCE] and from corrections sent by users. Hours, services and phone numbers change. We cannot promise that every entry is correct or up to date, so please phone ahead when you can. We are not responsible for decisions made from information in the app.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. If a change affects what the app does with your information, we will also say so in the app's update notes.
