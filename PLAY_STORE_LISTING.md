**Full description**

<!-- EN-FULL-START -->
```
Find a health facility near you, even without internet.

Afya Karibu is a free directory of more than 5,600 health facilities in Tanzania. Search for a facility, browse the map and see which ones are closest to you. Everything works offline, so it is useful where the signal is weak or mobile data is expensive.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Search for health facilities
• Browse a map with grouped markers that open up as you zoom in
• See facilities near you. Your location stays on your phone
• Suggest a correction or a new facility. Every suggestion is checked before it is published
• Use the app with no account, no ads and no analytics

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your location is used only on your phone and is never sent to us. What you search for stays on your phone. The app sends us something only when you choose to submit a correction.

IMPORTANT
Afya Karibu is a directory. It is not medical advice and it is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency services or go to the nearest health facility straight away. Opening hours, services and phone numbers change, so please phone ahead when you can.

Afya Karibu is an independent app published by DayBridge Enterprises Ltd. It is not a government service.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Questions or corrections: masumbasunday9@gmail.com
```

## Check against the app before pasting

1. **Facility count.** "More than 5,600" comes from the 5,651 facilities bundled on 24 September. Check the number in the current build.
2. **Features.** Confirm the app has search, a map with grouped markers, a nearby view and the suggestion form, exactly as described.
3. **Language of the app.** The listing does not say the app itself is in Swahili. If it is, add "Available in English and Swahili" to both descriptions.
4. **"Not a government service."** True if the app has no official partnership. If the Ministry of Health supplies the data under an agreement, ask them how they want that described. Never imply endorsement.
5. **Contact email.** Play shows it publicly. This is your personal Gmail. A dedicated address is cleaner, but it is your choice.
6. **Motion and new features.** If the modernization work adds features that collect data, update the policy and this listing.
